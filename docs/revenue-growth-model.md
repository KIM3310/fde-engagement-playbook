# Revenue Growth Model - fde-engagement-playbook

Updated: 2026-05-30

This note translates the archived proof into a current, ethical revenue path. It keeps the repository's archived status visible while preserving the commercial idea behind the work.

## Model

| Field | Decision |
|---|---|
| Revenue type | Archived proof to diagnostic, workshop, enablement, pilot, or partner package |
| Primary reader or buyer | AI consultancies, technical founders, field engineering teams, and implementation partners. |
| Wedge | Complex AI deployments often fail in discovery, acceptance criteria, stakeholder alignment, or handoff rather than model quality. |
| Operating note | Sell the revived workflow or advisory package; do not sell an unsupported archive as a live product. |

## Offer Ladder

| Offer | Price assumption | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Engagement design workshop | $5k-$18k | Turn a vague AI initiative into discovery, acceptance, and delivery artifacts. |
| FDE operating kit | $15k-$60k | Customize templates, cadence, and handoff rituals for one delivery organization. |
| Partner playbook license | $2k-$12k/month | Provide maintained templates, examples, and office hours for repeat engagements. |

## Activation Loop

- Traffic source: Founder and consulting playbooks.
- First action: read the README status note and one proof path that can be checked quickly.
- Conversion offer: Engagement design workshop.
- Expansion: convert a diagnostic or workshop into a scoped pilot, enablement pack, or maintained partner asset.

## Growth Experiments

- Create a paid diagnostic landing route
- Add a sample end-to-end engagement pack
- Bundle templates into an implementation partner offer

## Metrics

- Workshop close rate
- Template adoption
- Delivery cycle time
- Repeat partner revenue

## Ethical Boundary

- Do not sell process as a replacement for technical delivery
- Keep customer examples anonymized
- Avoid broad transformation promises
- Keep archived status visible in sales or portfolio conversations.
- Do not invent users, revenue, endorsements, certifications, or customer traction.
- Use buyer psychology only to reduce confusion and risk, not to pressure users.
