# Monetization Playbook - fde-engagement-playbook

This is a commercial planning artifact, not a revenue guarantee. It exists to turn an archived technical proof into a clear, ethical selling motion.

## Commercial Lane

- **Lane:** Field engineering engagement operating system
- **Primary buyer:** AI consultancies, technical founders, field engineering teams, and implementation partners.
- **Why this can sell:** Complex AI deployments often fail in discovery, acceptance criteria, stakeholder alignment, or handoff rather than model quality.

## Offer Ladder

1. **Engagement design workshop** - $5k-$18k. Turn a vague AI initiative into discovery, acceptance, and delivery artifacts.
2. **FDE operating kit** - $15k-$60k. Customize templates, cadence, and handoff rituals for one delivery organization.
3. **Partner playbook license** - $2k-$12k/month. Provide maintained templates, examples, and office hours for repeat engagements.

## Acquisition Channels

- Founder and consulting playbooks
- AI implementation partner outreach
- Case-study teardown content

## Proof Gates Before Selling

- Templates are complete and easy to copy
- Acceptance criteria are concrete
- Relationship to platform repos is visible

## Risk Boundaries

- Do not sell process as a replacement for technical delivery
- Keep customer examples anonymized
- Avoid broad transformation promises

## 30/60/90 Day GTM

- **30 days:** Create a paid diagnostic landing route
- **60 days:** Add a sample end-to-end engagement pack
- **90 days:** Bundle templates into an implementation partner offer

## Metrics That Matter

- Workshop close rate
- Template adoption
- Delivery cycle time
- Repeat partner revenue

## Positioning Note

Keep the repository's archived status visible. Sell the reusable workflow, diagnostic, enablement material, or pilot path that the proof makes credible; do not sell the archive as an unsupported production service.
